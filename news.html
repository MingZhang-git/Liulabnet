    Oncogenic fatty acid oxidation senses circadian disruption in sleep-deficiency-enhanced tumorigenesis

    Our findings reveal that fatty acid oxidation (FAO) is a circadian rhythm-sensoring mechanism that links sleep-deficiency (SD) to lung tumorigenesis and provides a promising chronotherapy strategy with β-endorphin for patients suffering from SD-related cancer.
